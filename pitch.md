# Pitch: Hell Run

Hell Run is a fast-paced, metal-themed roguelike rhythm runner for mobile. The player runs through Hell, blasting demons with guitar chords synchronized to heavy metal drums, dodging enemies, collecting loot, and defeating powerful bosses on the way to escaping Hell.

## Core loop
The player automatically runs forward through three lanes, switching between them to dodge monsters, collect items, and position themselves for attacks.

Monsters have different colors, each corresponding to a guitar chord. The player taps the matching chord button to fire a laser at monsters in their current lane. No aiming is required.

Chords can be played at any time, but playing in sync with the drum beat deals significantly more damage.

Monsters that reach the player force them into melee combat, damaging the guitar's durability. If the guitar breaks, the run ends.

Killing monsters and running generate Rage, which can be spent on powerful ultimate attacks.

As the player progresses, new chord colors, stronger enemies, more challenging patterns, and bosses are introduced. Boss fights can also feature Solo Mode, allowing players to perform rapid guitar sequences for massive damage.

## Meta
Players collect resources and special items dropped by monsters during runs.

Between runs, they can unlock and upgrade guitars with different durability, damage, and special abilities. Each guitar can offer a different playstyle, such as pitch bending by holding and dragging a chord button to deal additional damage.

Players can also unlock special attacks, improve their builds, and gradually progress through new areas of Hell.

The game uses procedurally arranged level sections and encounters, making each run different while maintaining a structured progression through Hell.

## Why come back?
Guitar collection and upgrades give players a reason to keep playing, experiment with different abilities and builds, and become powerful enough to reach deeper areas of Hell and eventually, escape.

Daily challenges offer special runs with different enemy combinations, modifiers, and rewards, encouraging players to return and improve their performance.

## Who and when
The game is designed for players who enjoy fast-paced arcade games, rhythm games, roguelikes, and heavy metal music.

It is played in portrait mode with one thumb, making it suitable for short sessions on the go.

The game targets meaningful 1–3 minute sessions for casual play, while successful runs can continue much longer as players progress through Hell.

## Feel
Hell Run should feel fast, aggressive, rhythmic, and satisfying.

Heavy metal drums establish the rhythm, while the guitar chords played by the player become part of the music. Perfectly timed chords trigger powerful lasers, explosive enemy deaths, and satisfying sound effects.

Screen shake, hit-stop, particles, and haptics emphasize powerful attacks. Dodging between lanes should feel fast and responsive, while Rage attacks and guitar solos create intense audiovisual moments.

The game uses pixel-art environments, demonic creatures, lava, and heavy metal aesthetics to create the atmosphere of Hell.

The goal is to make the player feel like they are performing an epic metal song while fighting through hordes of demons.
